About Us

Beyondance Co Ltd was established in 2019 and runs Beyond Dance Studio in Riccarton, Christchurch. From our studio at 35 Acheron Drive, we run a weekly timetable of hip hop, K-pop, jazz and jazz funk classes, as well as open classes and holiday programmes, for dancers of different ages and levels.

Alongside our classes, the Beyondance Crews give dancers opportunities to train at a higher level, perform on stage, compete in local, national and international competitions, and grow alongside a team of dedicated dancers and coaches.

“Be bold, be a little different, and go Beyond!” Beyond Dance Studio
